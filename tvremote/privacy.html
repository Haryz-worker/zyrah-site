TV Remote – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app TV Remote (package com.zyrah.tvremote), made by Zyrah Apps, uses, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the app only.

In short

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • The app talks to your TV directly over your own Wi-Fi. Your TV list and connection keys stay on your phone. We have no server that receives them.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID and other device data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in the app.

1. Information that stays on your phone

To work as a remote, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • the TVs you add: their name, model, IP address and network (MAC) address, which the app needs to connect and to turn the TV on over Wi-Fi;
  • the connection key your TV gives the app after you choose Allow, and the TV's security certificate fingerprint;
  • your settings (for example vibration), apps you pin, and simple counters such as how many times the app was opened, used to decide when an ad or a rating request may appear.

This data is not sent to us. It is not included in Android cloud backups. It is deleted when you remove a TV in the app or uninstall the app.

2. Communication with your TV

The app finds and controls TVs on your local network. It sends your key presses, touchpad movements, the text you type in the Keyboard tab, and app-launch requests to your TV only. Text you type is sent to the TV and is not stored by the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads and, at most once every 10 minutes, a full-screen ad at a natural break. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA and the UK).

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (or, if you have removed it, an app-set ID);
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown and taps, and diagnostics such as crashes inside the ads SDK.

Google acts as an independent controller of this data. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your ad choices

  • Consent. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested. Ads are loaded only after you make a choice. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads.
  • Change your choice. Open Settings › Ad privacy choices in the app.
  • Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads on most phones).
  • US state privacy laws. Where applicable, Google's form also lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising.

4. Rating the app

After a few good sessions the app may ask you to rate it, using Google Play's in-app review feature. The rating and review go to Google Play under your Google account, according to Google's terms. The app does not see what you write.

5. Feedback emails

If you tap Send feedback, the app opens your own email app with a message to support@the-zyrah.com. The message already includes the app version, Android version, phone model and TV model, so we can help faster. You can see and edit all of it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use your email only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not collect information that identifies you, other than what you choose to send us by email.

8. Data security and retention

Connections to your TV use encryption where your TV supports it (newer Samsung TVs), and the app checks the TV's certificate after the first connection. Data on your phone stays until you remove it or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and is not designed for them. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Because the app keeps your data on your phone, you can delete it yourself by uninstalling the app. For ad data, use the choices in section 3 or contact Google. For anything else, write to us.

TV Remote is an independent app. It is not made, endorsed or sponsored by Samsung Electronics. Samsung and Tizen are trademarks of their owners.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Important changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
